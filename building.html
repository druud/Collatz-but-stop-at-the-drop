Stop at the drop: the building

Every floor has one way out. An odd floor has a stair numbered 1, up to (3x+1)/2. An even floor has a hole with a pole numbered 0, down to x/2. Board on a floor and ride until the first floor below your start: the drop, where lunch is waiting.

Try:
Blue: stairs numbered 1. Orange: poles numbered 0. Dashed line: the floor you boarded; the ride ends at the first stop below it. Hyphens in the word sit at the lowest floors of the ride, counted from the first step on.
